Comparison uses matching identifiers and exact displayed cell values after trimming surrounding whitespace. An added or removed record generates one summary row; a changed record generates one row per changed field.

Select worksheets from Excel files or compare two CSV exports. Both files remain in your browser during processing.

Key integrity matters

Blank keys and duplicate IDs are explicitly excluded from unambiguous matching and reported as key issues.

Designed for auditing

Filter changed, added and removed records. Export a detailed CSV for review in Excel or another data tool.
